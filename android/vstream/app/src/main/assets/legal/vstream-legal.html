1. Terms of Use

  • Free channels. When you stream to Memoryq, Vstream picks a free live channel for you and gives you a stream key. Channels are shared: a channel you have not used for a while is given back, and when every channel is busy you may have to wait. We do not promise that a channel will always be available.
  • Fair use. Do not try to get around limits, use other people's keys, overload the service, or use Vstream to send anything other than your own live stream.
  • Ending. You can stop using Vstream and delete your account at any time. We may stop a stream, take back a channel, or suspend an account that breaks these terms or puts other people at risk.

3. Privacy

  • Camera and microphone. Vstream uses them only while you are previewing or live. While live, a notification is shown and the stream keeps running when the screen is off, until you tap Stop.
  • What goes where. When Memoryq is your destination, your video and sound go to memoryq.com and are shown publicly to anyone watching that channel. When you use your own server, they go only to that server; we do not see them.
  • What we keep. Your account details, the channel you used and when, your stream title, and the "also stream to" destinations you saved (the stream keys are stored on our server so we can send your stream on, and are never shown back in full).
  • Recordings and Recent streams. Streams on Memoryq channels are recorded on our server and kept for up to 30 days, then deleted automatically, unless a recording is kept as a video (for example on Memoryq TV). During those 30 days anyone can watch your stream again under Recent streams in Vstream, with its title and your username. You can remove your own recording at any time: Live streams → Recent streams → press and hold it. Streams to your own server are not recorded by us.
  • On your phone. Your sign-in, settings and your own-server key are kept in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Ads. Vstream is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the get-ready screen before you go live, and now and then a full-screen ad after a stream has ended. Never while you are live. Google may use your device's advertising ID, IP address and how you interact with ads to show and measure them. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland) we ask for your consent first; you can change it any time in Settings → Privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. More: how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • No selling. We do not sell your data.
  • Deleting. Remove a saved destination at any time in Settings. Deleting your Memoryq account deletes your destinations and channel claim. More in the Memoryq Privacy Policy.

4. Copyright and music

Stream only what you own or have permission to use. Playing commercial music, films, sports or TV in your stream usually needs a licence, and other platforms may mute or end streams that contain it. If you believe a stream on Memoryq infringes your copyright, send us the details (what the work is, the channel and time, and your contact information) at support@memoryq.com. We remove infringing streams and suspend repeat infringers.
