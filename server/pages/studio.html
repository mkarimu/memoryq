On air log

MP3, M4A, AAC, OGG, OPUS, FLAC or WAV, up to 60 MB each. Title and artist come from the song's tags (or a file name like "Artist - Title.mp3"). Add a link (Spotify, YouTube, your shop) so listeners can tap "Get this song". Only add music you own or have the rights to broadcast.

What plays when

Listener requests come first, then the show that is on, then jingles that are due, then your rotation playlists (by weight). Without playlists, all songs are shuffled.

Go live - station login

Broadcast live from your PC with free DJ software such as BUTT (broadcast using this tool) or Mixxx. When you connect, your music fades out and you are on air; when you disconnect, the playlist comes back by itself.

Give the password only to DJs you trust. A new password stops the old one from working.

Remote DJs

DJs who play from somewhere else - a club, a party, their home - each with their own login. When one connects, they are on air and AutoDJ waits in the background; when they stop, AutoDJ comes back by itself. One DJ is live at a time.

Setting up BUTT

Remote DJs use the same address, port and mount, with their own login as the user and their own password.

  1. Install BUTT from danielnoethen.de/butt, open Settings - Main - Server - Add.
  2. Type Icecast, and copy the address, port, password, mount and user from above.
  3. In Audio, choose MP3, 128 kbps, 44100 Hz, stereo. Pick your microphone or mixer as the input.
  4. Press Play to go live. Press Stop to hand back to the playlist.

Setting up Mixxx

  1. Open Preferences - Live Broadcasting, add a connection of type Icecast 2.
  2. Copy host, port, mount, login and password from above. Format MP3, 128 kbps, stereo.
  3. Tick Enable live broadcasting (or Options - Enable Live Broadcasting).
